# User Stories - AI Smart Camera & Auto-Capture App

## User Stories

1. **US-01: Webcam Stream Initialization**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want to** grant camera permissions and see a live video preview on the app dashboard,
   - **So that** I can frame the group photo properly.

2. **US-02: Real-Time Face & Gaze Detection**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want** the system to visually highlight detected faces and indicate whether each person is looking at the camera,
   - **So that** I know if everyone is paying attention before taking a photo.

3. **US-03: Automatic Photo Triggering**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want** the camera to automatically start a countdown timer (e.g., 3 seconds) when all people look at the camera simultaneously,
   - **So that** no manual button press is required.

4. **US-04: Audio & Visual Countdown Feedback**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want to** hear a sound effect and see an on-screen countdown,
   - **So that** everyone knows the exact moment the photo will be captured.

5. **US-05: Multi-Burst Capture & AI Quality Scoring**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want** the app to take a quick burst of photos and automatically pick the best shot based on open eyes and smiles,
   - **So that** I get the highest quality group picture.

6. **US-06: Instant Gallery & Preview**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want to** view a preview gallery of all captured photos from the current session,
   - **So that** I can inspect the results immediately.

7. **US-07: One-Click Photo Download**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want to** download the best shot or all captured photos in high resolution to my local device,
   - **So that** I can save and share them.

8. **US-08: Sensitivity & Timer Configuration**
   - **As a** user,
   - **I want to** adjust settings such as countdown length (3s, 5s) and gaze detection sensitivity,
   - **So that** the auto-capture adapts to different lighting conditions or group sizes.
