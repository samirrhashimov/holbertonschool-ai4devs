## Problem Statement
When taking group photos, people often look away, blink, or get distracted right as the image is captured. Additionally, the person taking the photo is usually left out of the shot or has to make multiple attempts to capture the perfect moment.

## MVP Features
- **Real-Time Gaze & Face Detection**: Real-time webcam tracking to detect faces and gaze direction.
- **Smart Auto-Trigger**: Automatically starts a 3-second capture timer when all detected individuals look at the camera and smile simultaneously.
- **AI Photo Quality & Smile Scoring**: Evaluates captured frames with AI to automatically highlight and select the best photo.
- **Instant Preview & Download**: Displays an instant preview of the captured photo with a one-click download option.
