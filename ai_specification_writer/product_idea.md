# Product Idea: PerfectSnap Camera

## Vision
Ensure group photos always turn out perfect by automatically triggering the camera only when every person is looking at the screen with their eyes open.

## Users
- **Group Photo Takers & Event Goers**: People who want to capture perfect group shots without multiple retakes or ruined photos due to blinked eyes.
- **Solo & Tripod Photographers**: Users taking timed group photos who cannot manually check if everyone is ready.

## Key Features
- **Real-Time Blink & Gaze Detection**: Monitors eye status (open/closed) and gaze direction for all detected faces in the frame.
- **Smart Auto-Capture**: Automatically triggers the shutter when 100% of detected people are looking at the camera with eyes open.
- **Burst Auto-Composite**: Captures a quick burst of frames and combines the best open-eye faces into one seamless photo if a single perfect shot is missed.
- **Visual/Audio Readiness Indicator**: Displays a subtle highlight (e.g., green frames around ready faces) so users know who isn't looking yet.
